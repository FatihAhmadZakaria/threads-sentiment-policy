<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

<p class="updated">
  Effective Date: November 8, 2026
</p>

<p>
  This Privacy Policy describes how ThreadsSentimentCollector
  ("the Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects
  information obtained through the Threads API.
</p>

<p>
  ThreadsSentimentCollector is an independent academic
  research application developed for collecting publicly
  available Threads posts based on selected hashtags
  for sentiment classification research.
</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>
  The Application may collect publicly available information
  from Threads posts, including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Public post text and captions.</li>
  <li>Post ID.</li>
  <li>Public username or account identifier, when provided by the API.</li>
  <li>Post publication timestamp.</li>
  <li>Post permalink.</li>
  <li>Hashtags or keywords used for searching.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  The Application does not intentionally access private
  posts, private messages, passwords, or restricted account
  information.
</p>

<h2>2. Purpose of Data Collection</h2>

<p>
  Collected information is used exclusively for academic
  and research purposes, including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Building datasets from publicly available Threads posts.</li>
  <li>Text preprocessing and data cleaning.</li>
  <li>Manual and assisted sentiment annotation.</li>
  <li>Developing sentiment classification models.</li>
  <li>Evaluating positive, negative, and neutral sentiment classification.</li>
  <li>Preparing academic reports and research publications.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  The collected information will not be sold or used for
  commercial advertising purposes.
</p>

<h2>3. Data Storage and Security</h2>

<p>
  Research datasets are stored locally on the researcher's
  computer or personal storage devices.
</p>

<p>
  The Application does not operate a public database
  containing collected Threads posts.
</p>

<p>
  Reasonable security measures are applied to protect
  collected data and API credentials from unauthorized access.
</p>

<h2>4. Data Sharing</h2>

<p>
  The Application does not sell, rent, or distribute
  collected personal information to third parties.
</p>

<p>
  Research findings may be presented in academic papers,
  reports, or presentations. Where possible, results will
  be presented in aggregated or anonymized form.
</p>

<h2>5. Data Retention</h2>

<p>
  Collected data is retained only for as long as necessary
  to complete the academic research and related evaluation.
</p>

<p>
  Data that is no longer required will be deleted from
  active research datasets and local storage under the
  researcher's control, subject to applicable legal and
  platform requirements.
</p>

<h2>6. Data Deletion Requests</h2>

<p>
  Individuals who believe their public Threads content
  has been included in the research dataset may request
  its removal.
</p>

<p>
  Requests can be submitted by email to:
</p>

<p>
  <strong>
    <a href="mailto:fatihahmadzakaria@gmail.com">
      fatihahmadzakaria@gmail.com
    </a>
  </strong>
</p>

<p>
  To help identify the relevant content, please provide
  the Threads post URL or post ID and a brief explanation
  of the request.
</p>

<p>
  The researcher will review valid requests and, where
  the relevant record can be identified and removal is
  permitted, delete it from the active local research
  dataset and discontinue its use in future analysis.
  Requests will normally be addressed within 30 days.
</p>

<p>
  This process only applies to copies controlled by the
  researcher. It does not delete the original content
  from Threads or Meta's systems.
</p>

<h2>7. Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>

  to retrieve publicly available information.
</p>

<p>
  Use of the Threads platform and its API is subject
  to Meta's applicable terms, developer policies, and
  privacy requirements.
</p>

<p>
  ThreadsSentimentCollector is an independent research
  application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by,

</p>

<p>

  has been included in the research dataset, please
  contact the researcher for review and appropriate action.
</p>
